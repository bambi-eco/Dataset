### Within flights

On 178 flights with at least five boxes of each kind, the mean canopy fraction under half-visible boxes exceeds that under fully visible boxes on 142 flights (80%). Median difference +0.319 (IQR +0.031 to +0.569); Wilcoxon signed-rank p = 7e-23.

### Logistic regression, cluster-robust by flight

Model 1: `occluded ~ canopy_in_box + species`, where canopy_in_box is the fraction of the box under the tree cover mask

- canopy in box: odds ratio 19.94 (95% CI 13.04–30.48), p = 2.1e-43; McFadden pseudo-R² 0.406

Model 2: adds `window_canopy`, the canopy fraction of the 256 px window around the box centre, plus deadwood and snow in the box and log box area

- canopy: OR 2.35 (1.52–3.64), p = 0.00012
- window_canopy: OR 13.10 (6.35–27.04), p = 3.5e-12
- deadwood_box: OR 4.22 (1.99–8.97), p = 0.00018
- snow_box: OR 0.22 (0.08–0.58), p = 0.0024
- box_area: OR 1.18 (0.82–1.70), p = 0.36
- McFadden pseudo-R² 0.432

Among boxes that touch canopy, Spearman ρ between half-visibility and canopy fraction in the 32 px ring around the box: 0.247 (p = 0).
